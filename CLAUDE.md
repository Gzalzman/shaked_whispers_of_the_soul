# CLAUDE.md

## Project Overview

This project is a Hebrew RTL landing page for Sound Healing workshops.

The landing page should feel soft, emotional, warm and spacious.
It is not a hard-sales page. It should create a sense of calm, safety, depth, inner listening and gentle transformation.

The page is inspired by existing Sound Healing / retreat landing pages, but must not copy their design, text, images or exact structure.
Use the reference only for atmosphere, flow and content hierarchy.

## Main Goal

Create a beautiful, modern and conversion-focused landing page for Sound Healing workshops.

The visitor should immediately understand:

* What the workshop is
* Who it is for
* What they will experience
* Who facilitates it
* When and where it happens
* How much it costs
* How to register or send a WhatsApp inquiry

## Design Direction

Use a soft, warm and calming visual language.

### Visual Style

* Rounded images
* Soft organic shapes
* Gentle spacing
* Calm emotional design
* Warm natural atmosphere
* Elegant but not overly spiritual
* Clean, premium and human
* Avoid heavy, aggressive or “salesy” design

### Colors

Use soft colors that fit Sound Healing:

* Warm beige
* Soft sand
* Cream
* Light clay
* Muted peach
* Pale lavender
* Gentle sage green
* Soft brown accents
* Optional subtle gold accents

Avoid:

* Strong neon colors
* Harsh contrast
* Black-heavy sections
* Loud gradients
* Overly mystical / dark visual style

### Typography

* Hebrew RTL layout
* Large emotional headings
* Short readable paragraphs
* Spacious line-height
* Clear hierarchy
* Mobile-first readability

Use a calm, modern Hebrew-friendly font stack.

## Image Guidelines

Images are very important in this landing page.

Use:

* Rounded image corners
* Large atmospheric hero image
* Small image clusters throughout the page
* Soft shadows
* Organic image positioning
* Images of instruments, bowls, gongs, calm spaces, workshop atmosphere, people resting, circles, candles only if subtle

Do not use:

* Sharp square image blocks
* Too many candles
* Anything that feels like a ritual or cult
* Overly dark spiritual visuals
* Stock photos that feel fake or corporate

Images should feel real, warm and grounded.

## Page Structure

Build the landing page with the following sections.

### 1. Hero Section

The first screen should immediately create emotion.

Content direction:

* Main headline about stopping, listening, breathing, softening or returning inward
* Short subheadline explaining that this is a Sound Healing workshop
* Date, location and short practical detail
* Main CTA button to WhatsApp or registration
* Soft rounded image or image collage

Possible headline style:

“לעצור. להקשיב. לחזור פנימה.”

Alternative:

“מרחב של צלילים, שקט ונשימה”

Hero should include:

* Workshop name
* Date
* Location
* Short promise
* CTA button

### 2. Emotional Intro Section

Explain the experience in simple, emotional Hebrew.

Focus on:

* The body softening
* The nervous system relaxing
* A pause from daily noise
* Letting the sound carry the process
* No need for previous experience

Keep the text short and spacious.

### 3. What Happens in the Workshop

Use cards or soft content blocks.

Include:

* Opening circle
* Gentle grounding / breathing
* Sound Healing session
* Integration / sharing
* Time for rest and silence

Use icons only if they are subtle and elegant.

### 4. Instruments Section

Show the instruments that may be used.

Examples:

* Tibetan bowls
* Crystal bowls
* Gong
* Chimes
* Handpan
* Drums
* Soft percussion
* Voice / humming if relevant

This section should be visual and atmospheric.

### 5. Who Is It For

Use a soft list.

Examples:

* People who feel overwhelmed
* People who need quiet
* People who want to reconnect to themselves
* People who find it hard to stop
* People curious about sound healing
* No previous experience required

Avoid making medical claims.

Do not promise healing, treatment or cure.

### 6. Facilitator Section

Create a warm “About the Facilitator” section.

Include:

* Rounded portrait image
* Short personal introduction
* Professional background
* Why this work matters
* Human tone

If there are multiple facilitators, use two rounded cards.

### 7. Practical Details Section

Include clear practical information:

* Date
* Time
* Location
* Duration
* What to bring
* What is included
* Number of participants if limited
* Accessibility / parking if relevant

Use a clean card layout.

### 8. Pricing Section

Pricing should be clear, calm and easy to understand.

Include:

* Early registration price
* Regular price
* Couple price if relevant
* Payment instructions
* Limited places note

The pricing section should not feel aggressive.

### 9. Gallery / Moments Section

Add a visual section with rounded images.

The purpose is to create trust and atmosphere.

Images should show:

* Workshop setting
* Instruments
* Soft group space
* Calm human presence

### 10. FAQ Section

Add frequently asked questions.

Suggested questions:

* האם צריך ניסיון קודם?
* מה לובשים?
* האם זה מתאים גם למי שלא מכיר סאונדהילינג?
* האם זו סדנה טיפולית?
* מה קורה אם אני נרדם/ת?
* כמה אנשים יהיו?
* איך נרשמים?

Tone should be reassuring and simple.

### 11. Final CTA Section

End with a strong but soft closing.

Example direction:

“אם הגוף מבקש רגע של שקט — זה המקום לעצור.”

Include CTA button:

* “שלחו לי פרטים בווטסאפ”
* “אני רוצה להירשם”
* “בדיקת מקום פנוי”

## Copywriting Tone

Use Hebrew copy that feels:

* Warm
* Human
* Grounded
* Emotional
* Simple
* Not too mystical
* Not too clinical
* Not too commercial

Avoid:

* Overpromising
* Medical claims
* Words like “ריפוי מובטח”
* Too much spiritual jargon
* Long paragraphs
* Corporate marketing language

Preferred words:

* שקט
* רכות
* נשימה
* הקשבה
* מרחב
* חזרה פנימה
* הרפיה
* נוכחות
* צלילים
* מנוחה
* גוף
* לב
* קרקוע

“היי, אשמח לקבל פרטים נוספים על סדנת הסאונדהילינג”

“היי, אשמח לקבל פרטים נוספים על סדנת הסאונדהילינג בתאריך [DATE]”
